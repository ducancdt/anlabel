ANLABEL TRIAL 7 NGAY VA KICH HOAT OFFLINE
=========================================

CHO KHACH HANG
1. Cai dat ANLAbel Trial bang file Setup x64.
2. Ban dung thu bat dau o lan mo dau tien va co hieu luc dung 7 ngay.
3. Bam nut "Kich hoat" tren thanh tren cung de xem va sao chep Ma may.
4. Gui Ma may cho nha cung cap.
5. Dan Key kich hoat duoc cap vao cua so Kich hoat va bam KICH HOAT.
6. Co the kich hoat khi dang con han hoac sau khi het 7 ngay.

CHO CHU PHAN MEM
1. KHONG gui thu muc/file ANLAbel License Master cho khach hang.
2. Mo ANLAbel License Master tren dung tai khoan Windows cua chu phan mem.
3. Dan Ma may khach hang, nhap ten khach hang.
4. Chon key vinh vien hoac bo chon va dat ngay het han.
5. Bam TAO KEY KICH HOAT; key duoc tu dong sao chep.
6. Gui duy nhat chuoi key cho dung khach hang co Ma may tuong ung.

BAO MAT
- Key gan voi mot Ma may, khong dung duoc tren may khac.
- Key duoc ky so ECDSA; sua bat ky ky tu nao se bi tu choi.
- Khoa rieng Master duoc Windows ma hoa cho tai khoan hien tai.
- Neu cai lai Windows/mat tai khoan chu phan mem, Master key co the khong mo duoc.
- Khong cong khai source hoac bo cai License Master.
